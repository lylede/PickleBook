How booking works

Three steps. Most players finish in about a minute.

1. Pick a court

Browse partner facilities and compare hourly rates and opening hours.

2. Choose your time

Open hours are shown live. Tap a start time and how long you want to play.

3. Pay with GCash

Pay the facility's GCash QR and upload your receipt. The owner confirms your booking.

For court owners

Take bookings online instead of through chat.

Players see your schedule and rates before they ask. You confirm payments and manage courts from one dashboard.
